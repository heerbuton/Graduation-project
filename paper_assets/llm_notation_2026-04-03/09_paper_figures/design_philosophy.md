# Semantic Blueprint

This visual system treats algorithmic evidence as something that should feel observed rather than merely described. The page is pure white, the modules sit in careful alignment, and every block carries the quiet authority of an instrument panel built for close reading. Space does most of the explanatory work. The composition should feel meticulously crafted, restrained, and deliberate enough that a reader can understand the semantic chain at a glance while still sensing the painstaking attention behind each placement.

Color exists to separate logic, not to decorate. Deep graphite anchors the structure, a cool blue marks the forward semantic path, green marks successful validation, and muted red is reserved for failure and repair. Because the work must read in print after reduction, contrast is sharp, labels are large, and every edge, arrow, and code fragment is tuned with master-level precision. The result should look like the product of deep expertise and countless careful refinements rather than a software screenshot dressed up as a figure.

Form follows semantic hierarchy. Inputs appear as modular cards, knowledge appears as a compact table, generation appears as a controlled model chamber, validation appears as a gate with branching consequence, and output appears as disciplined code or notation. Nothing floats casually. Each module should feel painstakingly aligned, evenly weighted, and part of a single visual grammar. Repetition is intentional: repeated radii, repeated border weights, repeated spacing intervals. That consistency is what gives the figures their scholarly calm.

The figures should never feel theatrical. They should feel precise, readable, and labored over by someone at the top of their field. Even when showing a repair loop or a rendered score, the emphasis remains on semantic transformation and evidentiary traceability. The finished images must look meticulously crafted, publication-ready, and coherent enough to sit together as one visual family across the method and experiment chapters.
